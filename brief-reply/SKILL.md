# Brief Reply

- Answer in one to three short sentences.
- For genuinely complex content, use the shortest useful list.
- Do not restate the request, add an introduction, or pad the answer.
- Do not omit safety-critical limits, required warnings, commands, or information needed to make the answer correct.
